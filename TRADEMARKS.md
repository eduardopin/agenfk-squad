similar to them are marks of Eduardo José Pin. The [license](LICENSE) covers
the code; it grants no right to these marks (see its Trademarks clause).

This policy says what you may do with the name without asking.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that your work uses, works with, or is based on agenfk-squad, in
  plain text: "built on agenfk-squad", "a fork of agenfk-squad".
- Naming the project in articles, talks, reviews, courses and documentation.
- Keeping the name in a fork that you use privately, or that exists only to
  send changes back to this repository through pull requests.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Publishing, distributing or offering a fork, a modified version or a
  derivative work under the name "agenfk-squad", or under a name or logo that
  could be confused with it.
- Using the name or logo in the name of a product, service, company, domain,
  package or repository other than this one.
- Suggesting that the Maintainer endorses, sponsors or maintains your work.

If you publish a fork, give it a different name and say where it comes from:
"based on agenfk-squad by Eduardo José Pin".

## AgenFK

AgenFK is a separate project by CG/lab, with its own name. This policy says
nothing about it.
