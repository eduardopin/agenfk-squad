Measured on 2026-10-02 with the 03:00 snapshot. The title states the finding; the body shows how to get there.

For: infrastructure on-call. Status: measured in staging, not measured in production.

A snapshot older than 24 hours loses the day's writes: check its date before you start.

Restoring is one command per step

aws rds restore-db-instance-from-db-snapshot \
  --db-instance-identifier staging-restore \
  --db-snapshot-identifier <SNAPSHOT_ID> --profile <RO_PROFILE>
